(01) Overview

RagForge is a production-grade RAG pipeline that combines hybrid sparse and dense retrieval with cross-encoder reranking to improve answer relevance, and measures every change with a statistical evaluation framework.

Hybrid retrieval

Sparse search catches exact identifiers and rare terms; dense search catches paraphrases. Fusing the two covers each one's blind spots.

Cross-encoder reranking

Candidates are rescored with the query and passage read together, trading speed on a short list for precision at the top.

Adaptive query routing

Simple lookups and open questions need different strategies, so the pipeline picks the route per query.

Hierarchical summarization and evaluation

Long corpora are summarized in layers, and retrieval and answer quality are checked with statistical tests, not eyeballing.

Interactive · runs in your browser
