(01) Overview

My capstone: multi-node classroom presence verification using ESP32 nodes. They read BLE signal strength (RSSI), report over Wi-Fi, and a set of rule-based checks decides whether a device is really in the room.

Multiple vantage points

Several ESP32 nodes hear the same device, so no single noisy reading decides the result.

RSSI as evidence

Signal strength is noisy, so it is treated as evidence to combine, not as a precise distance.

Wi-Fi reporting

Nodes send their readings to a collector over Wi-Fi.

Rule-based checks

Explicit rules turn smoothed readings into a present / not-present verdict you can inspect and tune.

Interactive · signal model

Signal lab

Three ESP32 nodes measure the signal strength (RSSI) of a student's BLE device. Drag the device around the room and out of the door. The lab adds realistic noise, smooths it, estimates the position by trilateration and applies the rule checks. Turn the noise up to see why rules and smoothing matter.
