(01) Overview

My final-year project: a deep learning-based real-time recommender. A learned model scores candidates, heuristic ranking refines the final list, and an inference pipeline serves it per request.

Learned preferences

A neural model learns user and item representations from interaction data instead of hand-written rules.

Heuristic ranking

Rules re-order the model's scores for the final list: diversity, popularity and business logic.

Inference pipeline

A serving path built for real-time requests, separate from training.

Both worlds

Learned scores plus transparent heuristics: accurate, and still explainable.

Interactive · trains in your browser

Training lab

A small matrix-factorization model, standing in for the deep model, learns the tastes of 24 synthetic users over 32 items in four genres. Watch the error fall as it trains, see items of the same genre cluster in the learned space, then get recommendations and re-rank them with heuristics.
